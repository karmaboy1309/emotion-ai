🏗️

Architecture

How the system processes emotions

EmotionAI uses a two-stage pipeline. First, MTCNN (Multi-task Cascaded Convolutional Network) detects faces in the input image or video frame with high precision, including facial landmark alignment.

Second, each detected face region is passed through a Mini-Xception CNN model trained on the FER2013 dataset to classify the emotion into one of 7 categories: angry, disgust, fear, happy, neutral, sad, and surprise.

The model outputs a probability distribution across all 7 emotions, giving you a confidence score for each detected emotion.
